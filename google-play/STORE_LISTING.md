```markdown
Hledáte odpověď na otázku, kdo je pro vás „švagrová bratrance“, „synovec z druhého kolena“ nebo jaký je rozdíl mezi „ujcem“ a „strynou“?

• Podpora 25 světových jazyků: Přepínejte mezi češtinou, slovenštinou, polštinou, němčinou, angličtinou a dalšími 20 jazyky.
```

```markdown
Wondering what your "mother's brother's daughter" is called, or how to explain complex in-law and step-family relationships?

Family Kinship Calculator is a clean, modern, and 100% ad-free genealogy calculator inspired by the traditional kinship terms calculator. Simply tap out your family relationship chain step-by-step on an ergonomic calculator keypad and instantly discover the precise kinship term, generational distance, civil degree of consanguinity, and estimated shared DNA percentage.

⭐ 100% AD-FREE & PRIVACY-FIRST
We respect your privacy. Zero ads, zero tracking SDKs, zero telemetry. The app operates completely offline directly on your device.

🌳 KEY FEATURES:
• Tactile Calculator Keypad: Step-by-step entry for Father, Mother, Brother, Sister, Son, Daughter, Husband, and Wife.
• Intelligent Reduction Rules: Automatically resolves compound chains into clean terms (e.g., Brother ➔ Son ➔ Mother reduces to Sister-in-law).
• Relationship Modifiers:
  - ⇧ Step Mode: Step-parents, step-children, half-siblings, and step-siblings.
  - ✝ Godparent Mode: Godfathers, godmothers, and godchildren.
• Mutual Query (Reciprocal Mode): Tap one button to swap perspective and see what that relative calls you.
• Visual Family Mini-Tree: Interactive graphical diagram displaying avatars and connecting relationship paths.
• GEDCOM Family Tree Import (.ged): Load genealogies from Ancestry, MyHeritage, or Gramps, analyze the shortest path between any two individuals, and transfer the path directly to the calculator.
• Consanguinity & DNA %: Canonical civil degree calculations and estimated shared DNA percentage.
• Educational Kinship Quiz: Sharpen your family tree vocabulary with dynamic multiple-choice challenges.
• Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Voice Dictation.
• Social PNG Card Generator: Export dark-mode graphical relationship cards to share with your family.
• 25 Fully Localized Languages: Czech, Slovak, Polish, German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and more.

Explore your family ties with precision and ease!
```
